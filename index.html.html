How it works

Your browsing boundaries,
powered by AI

Unlike blunt content blockers, SafeScroll reads in context — the way you do.

1
✍️

Set your personal filters

Type anything — "betting odds in sports", "calorie counts", "show spoilers" — however specific. Your filters are yours alone, stored only on your device.

2
🤖

AI reads in context

SafeScroll goes beyond keyword matching. It catches euphemisms, indirect references, and implied meaning — so a betting promotion gets filtered even if it never says "gambling".

3
🛡️

Choose your comfort setting

Warn — a gentle heads-up you can dismiss. Blur — hidden until you choose to look. Hide — replaced entirely. You set the level.
